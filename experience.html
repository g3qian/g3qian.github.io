Experience

Engineering at LPI, doctoral and postdoctoral research at UC San Diego, and teaching and mentoring in structural engineering.

My experience moves from the lab to the field: doctoral and postdoctoral research at UC San Diego, then engineering practice in Germany. Along the way, teaching and mentoring became an important part of the work. The most recent role comes first.

Hannover, Germany

R&D Engineer

LPI Ingenieurgesellschaft mbH

At LPI, I brought my research background to bridges in service, working on structural monitoring and condition assessment.

  • Automated operational modal analysis of long-term monitoring data, with bootstrap uncertainty bounds.
  • Studied how temperature shifts the natural frequencies of two twin bridges, one with documented tendon damage.
  • Interpreted strain, displacement, inclination, acoustic-emission, and fiber-optic measurements for bridge owners, and automated parametric SOFiSTiK models with Python.

Teaching, Mentoring, and Service

2021–2023

UC San Diego

Teaching

Teaching assistant for SE 168/268, Structural System Testing and Model Correlation (Spring 2022 and 2023), a course co-taught by Prof. Michael Todd and engineers from ATA Engineering. Over ten weeks, students take a plate from a finite-element model to a modal test, signal processing, test–model correlation, and model updating.

I led lab instruction in instrumentation, hammer and shaker testing, data acquisition, and data quality, and guided students through modal identification and correlation. In the final project, students locate a hidden spring and estimate its stiffness from new measurements.

I also supported SE 130A/B, Structural Analysis I and II (Summer 2021).
